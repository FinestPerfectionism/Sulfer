## §1 Behavior
### §1.1 Advertisement
You may not advertise anywhere throughout the guild.
**Example:**
> "plz join my serverrrrr and boooosttt plz plz plz"

### §1.2 Harassment
Any form of harassment, threats, or intimidation is forbidden.
**Example:**
> "imma kill you if you don't give tell me"

### §1.3 Discrimination
Racism, sexism, ableism, or other discriminatory behavior is prohibited.
**Example:**
> "you're so acoustic"

### §1.4 NSFW Content
Sharing explicit content is strictly prohibited in all channels unless explicitly marked NSFW. (Looks like we have none, so no NSFW :])
**Example:**
> "Here's the link to my special cam 😘🥵: …"

## §2 Language
### §2.1 No Loophole Language
Attempting to bypass rules through euphemisms, coded language, or indirect references is forbidden.
**Example:**
> "-.-- --- ..- .----. .-. . / ... ..- -.-. …. / .- / ... .. .-.. .-.. -. -. / .-.. .. - - .-.. . / -... .-.. ..- -.. / ..-. --- .-. / - .-. .- -. ... .-.. .- + .. -. --. / - …. .. …"

### §2.2 Spam
Repetitive messages, excessive emojis, pinging, or automated scripts are prohibited.
**Example:**
> "hello hello hello hello hello hello hello hello hello hello hello hello hello hello hello hello hello hello hello hello"

## §3 Privacy and Safety
### §3.1 Doxxing
Sharing personal information without explicit consent is banned.
**Example:**
> "his name is walter hartwell white he lives at 308 negra arroyo lane, albuquerque, new mexico, 87104."

### §3.2 Impersonation
Pretending to be staff, members, or external figures is not allowed.
**Example:**
> "im totally BW | 𝙸𝚗𝚏𝚒𝚗𝚒𝚞𝚖³"

## §4 Channel Use
### §4.1 Channel Specific Rules
All content must follow the designated purpose of each channel. Off-topic content is prohibited.
**Example:**
> LMAO LOOK AT THIS MEME I FOUND *While being in a bug report thread.*
